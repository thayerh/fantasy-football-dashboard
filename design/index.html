Why the palette behaves the way it does

Measured contrast ratios, not assumptions. This single table drove every layout decision above.

ForegroundOnRatioBody textLarge text
Carolina BlueWhite3.00FailPass
Carolina BlueCloud2.72FailFail
Carolina BlueNavy4.84PassPass
NavyWhite14.52PassPass
Bolin CreekWhite8.18PassPass
Old Well BasinWhite5.87PassPass
Flat GrayWhite2.36FailFail

The consequence: on light backgrounds Carolina Blue is a surface and accent colour, never body text. Body text is Navy or Bolin Creek. On navy, Carolina Blue becomes a legitimate text colour. UNC's own guidance agrees — it requires Carolina Blue text to be 24px, or 19px bold.

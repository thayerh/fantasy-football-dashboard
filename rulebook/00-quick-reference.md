## Waivers

**FAAB, $100/year, blind bid, minimum $0.** Continuous, daily processing at **3:00 AM ET**, 2-day waiver period. Budgets reset at the rookie draft and **do not roll over**. Ties broken by worse standing.

## Season

| | |
|---|---|
| Regular season | Weeks 1–14 |
| Trade deadline | Week 12, first kickoff |
| Playoffs | Weeks 15–17, 6 teams, top 2 seeds bye |
| Placement games | 5th place Wk 16 · 3rd place Wk 17 |

| Toilet bowl | Weeks 15–17, 6 teams, **losers advance** |

**Toilet bowl:** the six non-playoff teams. Lose and you advance. The team that loses the Week 17 final takes the **Willie Barron Trophy - Brought to you by The Pitch**. Full, legal, healthy lineups are still mandatory — you cannot lose on purpose.

## Startup Draft

**20 rounds · Snake · Live, one sitting, 2h30m max · Tiered clock**

`R1–3: 90s · R4–10: 35s · R11–20: 15s` — no break, one sitting. 240 picks, 2h 13m worst case.

Order set by the commissioner and **not tradeable**. Remaining 3 roster spots filled by FAAB before Week 1. **Queue 45+ players beforehand.**

## Rookie Draft

**4 rounds · Linear (not snake) · Live, ~1 hour · 90-second flat clock**

Date is set each year by league availability poll, within 4 weeks of the NFL Draft.

Picks 1–6: non-playoff teams in **reverse standings order** (set by Week 14, unaffected by the toilet bowl).

Picks 7–12: playoff teams. **Winner of each placement game gets the better pick** — 5th place game winner 1.07, loser 1.08; 3rd place game winner 1.09, loser 1.10; runner-up 1.11; champion 1.12.

## Trades

Process automatically. **No league vetoes.** Reversible by the commissioner only for collusion.

Tradeable: players, rookie picks (current year + 2).
Not tradeable: **FAAB**, cash, loans, future considerations, anything off-platform.

**Trading is closed until the startup draft ends.** No exceptions, including draft slots.

**You must have paid your dues to trade a future pick.**

**You must make at least one trade each season to be eligible for a payout.** One completed trade with another team, any time from February 1 through the Week 12 deadline. Sham trades do not count.

## Money

Dues **$25** in year one, **+$10 every year after**. Due **March 1**, by Venmo or Zelle.

Pool $300 → champion **$180** (60%) · runner-up **$75** (25%) · most points **$45** (15%). Paid within 14 days of the championship.

**No trade that season, no payout** — the share passes to the next eligible team.

## The Four Deadlines You Will Actually Miss

1. **March 1** — dues
2. **Rookie draft start** — set your queue
3. **Week 1 kickoff** — taxi squad locks (**Week 2 in year one**), and rosters must be legal *every* year
4. **Week 12 kickoff** — trades close, and your one required trade must be done

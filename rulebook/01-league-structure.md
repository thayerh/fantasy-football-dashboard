# 01 — League Structure

## 1.1 Format

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Teams | 12 |
| Type | Dynasty (full roster retention, no annual redraft) |
| Lineup | Superflex |
| Scoring | Full PPR (1.0 per reception) |
| Player pool | Offense only — QB, RB, WR, TE |
| Platform | Sleeper |
| League year | February 1 – January 31 |

## 1.2 Ownership

**One team, one owner.** Co-ownership is not permitted. A team is owned by exactly one person, who is solely responsible for that team's lineups, transactions, dues, and conduct.

An owner may not hold an ownership interest in any other team in this league, directly or through a family member or household member. If two owners share a household, they must disclose it to the commissioner before the startup draft; the commissioner may permit it but both owners are subject to heightened trade review (see [Trades §8.6](08-trades.md#86-collusion)).

Owners are expected to run their own team. Handing your account credentials to another owner, or having someone else set your lineup for more than an occasional emergency, is a violation.

## 1.3 The Commissioner

The commissioner is **Thayer Hicks**.

The commissioner:

- Administers the Sleeper league and keeps its settings matched to this document.
- Sets and enforces the league calendar.
- Collects dues and distributes payouts.
- Rules on disputes not clearly answered by this document.
- Investigates suspected collusion, tanking, and abandonment.
- Fills vacant teams.

The commissioner **also owns a team** and is bound by every rule in this document.

**Commissioner accountability.** The commissioner may not use commissioner tools to alter their own roster, force a transaction involving their own team, or reverse a result affecting their own team. Any such action requires a league vote. Commissioner trades are subject to the same review as everyone else's, with the review conducted by the co-commissioner.

**Co-commissioner.** A second owner, **Sawyer Fotheringham**, serves as co-commissioner. Their role is to review the commissioner's own trades, hold a backup set of platform credentials, and act as commissioner if the commissioner is unavailable or has a conflict of interest.

**Removal.** The commissioner may be removed by a vote of 9 of the 12 owners. See [Governance §9.2](09-governance-and-conduct.md#92-voting).

## 1.4 Dues

Dues **start at $25** for the inaugural season and **rise by $10 every year thereafter**.

| Season | Dues | Prize pool |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | $25 | $300 |
| Year 2 | $35 | $420 |
| Year 3 | $45 | $540 |
| Year 4 | $55 | $660 |
| *Each year after* | *+$10* | *+$120* |

The escalator is the point, not an accident. A $25 buy-in is low enough that nobody hesitates to join a league that has not proven itself yet, and the annual increase means the stakes grow alongside the rosters — by the time your team is five years of drafting and trading deep, there is real money on it. It also functions as a soft commitment test: an owner who balks at the increase is usually an owner who was already drifting away.

**Terms.**

- Dues are paid to the commissioner by **Venmo, Zelle, or directly through the Sleeper app**.
- Dues are due by **March 1** each year, before the rookie draft and before the league year's meaningful roster decisions.
- **An owner who has not paid dues may not trade a future draft pick**, in either direction. This is the single most important dues rule in a dynasty league — future picks are only worth something if the team owing them is still paying to exist.
- An owner who has not paid by March 1 receives one written warning and seven days. After that the team is declared vacant and handled under [Governance §9.5](09-governance-and-conduct.md#95-vacant-teams-orphans).
- Dues are non-refundable once the rookie draft has begun.

**Startup year exception:** dues for the inaugural season are due before the startup draft begins. No owner picks until every owner has paid.

## 1.5 Payouts

The prize pool is 100% of collected dues — **$300 in year one**, growing by $120 each season as dues escalate. The commissioner takes no cut.

| Finish | Share | Year 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Champion | 60% | **$180** |
| Runner-up | 25% | **$75** |
| Most regular-season points | 15% | **$45** |

"Most regular-season points" is close to universal in dynasty leagues. It pays the team that got unlucky rather than bad, and it is one of the few payouts a tanking team cannot chase.

**Eligibility — you must trade.** An owner who has not completed at least one trade during the season is **not eligible for any payout**, regardless of where they finish. A forfeited share passes to the next eligible team in that category. The full rule, including what counts as a qualifying trade, is [Trades §8.8](08-trades.md#88-the-annual-trade-requirement).

**Payment timing.** Payouts are distributed within 14 days of the championship game.

## 1.6 League Communication

The official league channel is the **league group text**.

- Rule announcements, deadlines, and votes happen there. Anything announced there is considered received by every owner.
- Trade negotiations may happen anywhere, but the executed trade must go through Sleeper. Nothing agreed outside the platform is binding.
- Owners are expected to be reachable. See [Governance §9.4](09-governance-and-conduct.md#94-inactivity).

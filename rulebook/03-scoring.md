# 03 — Scoring

Full PPR. The table below is the complete scoring system; anything not listed scores zero.

## 3.1 Passing

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Passing yards | **0.04** per yard (1 pt per 25 yards) |
| Passing touchdown | **4** |
| Interception thrown | **−2** |
| 2-point conversion (pass) | **2** |

**Four points per passing touchdown**, not six. In a Superflex league the SUPERFLEX slot already does the work of making quarterbacks valuable; inflating their scoring on top of it would make QB not merely the most important position but the only one that matters.

## 3.2 Rushing

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Rushing yards | **0.1** per yard (1 pt per 10 yards) |
| Rushing touchdown | **6** |
| 2-point conversion (rush) | **2** |

## 3.3 Receiving

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Reception | **1.0** |
| Receiving yards | **0.1** per yard (1 pt per 10 yards) |
| Receiving touchdown | **6** |
| 2-point conversion (reception) | **2** |

**No tight end premium.** Every position scores 1.0 per reception. This keeps the league's scoring aligned with the widest available set of published rankings and ADP data, which matters more than usual in a startup year where nobody has league history to price players against.

## 3.4 Miscellaneous

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Fumble lost | **−2** |
| Fumble recovered for TD (own) | **6** |
| Special teams / return TD (by an offensive player) | **6** |

**No bonuses.** This league does not use yardage milestone bonuses (100-yard games, 300-yard passing games, long-TD bonuses). They add variance without adding decisions, and they make scoring harder to reason about in trades.

**No negative points for kneel-downs, sacks taken, or incompletions.**

## 3.5 Fractional Scoring

All scoring is to **two decimal places**. There are no ties in a matchup unless both teams score exactly the same to the hundredth — see [Schedule & Playoffs §4.4](04-schedule-and-playoffs.md#44-ties).

## 3.6 Stat Corrections

The league uses **Sleeper's final official stats**. Sleeper applies NFL stat corrections automatically, usually through the following Tuesday.

- Matchup results are **final when Sleeper marks the week complete**. A stat correction that arrives after that point does not change a result.
- The one exception: a correction that would change a **playoff** result is honored whenever it arrives, up until payouts are distributed. Championships are worth getting right.

## 3.7 Changing Scoring

Scoring changes require a **9-of-12 supermajority** and may only take effect the following season — never mid-season, and never after the rookie draft. See [Governance §9.2](09-governance-and-conduct.md#92-voting).

The reason for the high bar: in a dynasty league, scoring settings *are* asset values. Changing the passing TD value or adding TE premium retroactively reprices every roster in the league and rewards whoever saw it coming.

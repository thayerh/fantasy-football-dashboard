# 07 — Waivers & Free Agency

This league uses **FAAB** (Free Agent Acquisition Budget) — blind bidding — for all player acquisitions. There is no rolling waiver priority list.

## 7.1 FAAB Budget

Each team receives **$100** per league year.

- The budget is issued **at the conclusion of the rookie draft** and runs until the following rookie draft.
- In the startup year, budgets are issued at the conclusion of the [startup draft](05-startup-draft.md#55-after-the-draft).
- **Budgets do not roll over.** Unspent money is gone. Sleeper does not reset budgets automatically; the commissioner resets them manually each year.
- Minimum bid is **$0**. A $0 bid is a legitimate bid and competes normally.
- **FAAB may not be traded** in any form — see [Trades §8.4](08-trades.md#84-faab-is-not-tradeable).

The budget is **$100**, not a larger figure. Bids stay legible at a glance — "$14" reads instantly as 14% of your season. The cost is occasional tied bids, which §7.3 resolves.

## 7.2 How Waivers Work

**Every dropped or newly available player goes through waivers.** There is no free-agent free-for-all period; nothing in this league is claimed first-come, first-served, at any point in the year.

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Waiver type | FAAB, continuous |
| Waiver period | **2 days** |
| Processing time | **3:00 AM ET, daily** |
| Bid visibility | Blind — all bids hidden until processing |

- A dropped player is on waivers for 2 days, then processes at the next 3:00 AM run.
- You may submit claims on as many players as you like, and order them by priority. Sleeper processes your claims in your stated order and stops honoring them once you run out of budget or roster space.
- Every claim must include a corresponding drop if you are at your roster limit. A claim that would create an illegal roster fails.
- **Waivers run year-round**, including the offseason. There is no dead period.

## 7.3 Tiebreakers

If two teams bid the same amount on the same player, the tie is broken by:

1. **Worse current standing** (or, in the offseason, worse finish in the most recent season)
2. **Larger remaining FAAB balance**
3. **Coin flip by the commissioner**

Using standings as the first tiebreaker rather than a rolling priority is deliberate: it gives struggling teams a small, consistent edge on the wire without creating a priority list that owners game by claiming and dropping.

## 7.4 In-Season Cutoffs

- **Waivers continue through the end of the championship week (Week 17).** Teams eliminated from contention retain full access to the wire.
- There is no roster freeze for non-playoff teams. Picking up a promising young player in Week 16 for next year is a legitimate dynasty move and this league encourages it.
- However: acquiring players in order to **help or harm another team's playoff run** is a violation. See [Governance §9.3](09-governance-and-conduct.md#93-tanking-and-lineup-integrity).

## 7.5 Offseason Free Agency

The wire stays live all offseason on the same daily FAAB schedule, using the **prior year's** remaining budgets until the rookie draft resets them.

This means an owner who spends to zero in November has no way to claim a free agent in March. That is intentional — budget management in a dynasty league should account for the whole calendar, not just the fantasy season.

**Rookies who go undrafted in the rookie draft** enter the free agent pool when the draft concludes and are claimed through normal waivers.

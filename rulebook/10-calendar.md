# 10 — League Calendar

The dynasty year does not stop. This is the annual cycle.

## The Year at a Glance

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Immediately after the championship game** | Trading reopens. Rookie draft order is set and posted in the league group text. |
| **February 1** | New league year opens. Amendment proposals open. Dues invoiced. |
| **March 1** | **Dues deadline** ($25 in year one, +$10 each year). Amendment proposals close. |
| **Early–mid March** | NFL free agency. The busiest trading window of the year. |
| **Mid-March** | Amendment votes concluded; rulebook updated. |
| **Late April** | NFL Draft. |
| **Within 3 days of the NFL Draft** | Commissioner posts the rookie draft date poll. |
| **Within 4 weeks of the NFL Draft** | **Rookie draft.** Live, 4 rounds, linear, 90-second clock, ~1 hour. Date set annually by availability. |
| **Conclusion of rookie draft** | FAAB budgets reset to $100 for all teams. |
| **Late August** | Final roster compliance check. |
| **NFL Week 1 kickoff** | **Taxi squad deadline** (year one: Week 2). Rosters must be legal. Season begins. |
| **Weeks 1–14** | Regular season. Daily waivers at 3:00 AM ET. |
| **After Week 8** | Commissioner posts which teams have not yet made their required annual trade ([§8.8](08-trades.md#88-the-annual-trade-requirement)). |
| **Week 11** | Second and final reminder on the annual trade requirement. |
| **Week 12, first kickoff** | **Trade deadline.** Last chance to satisfy the annual trade requirement. |
| **Weeks 15–17** | Playoffs (6 teams, with 5th- and 3rd-place games for draft slots) and the **toilet bowl** (6 teams, losers advance). |
| **Week 17** | Championship. |
| **Within 14 days of championship** | Payouts distributed, to eligible teams only. |

## Deadlines That Actually Matter

These four are the ones that cause problems when missed:

1. **March 1 — dues.** Miss it and you cannot trade future picks, and after seven days your team is declared vacant. This is the deadline the whole dynasty economy rests on.
2. **Rookie draft night.** It is live and about an hour, and the date moves every year — vote in the poll and put it in your calendar the day it is announced. If you cannot make it, queue your players; autopick does not care that you were busy.
3. **Week 1 kickoff — taxi deadline.** After this moment no player can be moved *onto* taxi for the rest of the season. If you meant to stash a rookie, do it in August. **The inaugural season is the one exception** — year one's taxi deadline is Week 2 kickoff, because the startup draft leaves no offseason to sort a roster out. Rosters must still be legal at Week 1 either way.
4. **Week 12 first kickoff — trade deadline.** Sleeper enforces this automatically and the commissioner will not reopen it.

## Offseason Rhythm

Dynasty leagues die in June and July. Some things that keep them alive, none of which are rules:

- Post the rookie draft order the week it is set, with commentary.
- Do a group rookie-class watch or a mock draft in April.
- Publish a leaguewide power ranking after NFL free agency.
- Keep the league chat alive during the NFL Draft itself — it is the second-best night of the fantasy year.

## Commissioner's Annual Checklist

- [ ] Announce the rookie draft order in the group text as soon as the championship ends
- [ ] Post the rookie draft date poll within 3 days of the NFL Draft; announce the date with 10+ days' notice
- [ ] Collect dues by March 1; confirm every team in the league chat
- [ ] Run the amendment window; update the rulebook and the change log
- [ ] Reset all 12 FAAB budgets manually after the rookie draft — *Sleeper does not do this for you*
- [ ] Verify Sleeper settings against the [appendix](appendix-sleeper-settings.md) before Week 1
- [ ] Confirm all rosters legal at Week 1 kickoff
- [ ] Post the annual-trade-requirement status after Week 8 and again in Week 11
- [ ] Post the toilet bowl bracket in the group text before Week 15 and track it weekly
- [ ] Confirm eliminated playoff teams have Week 16 / Week 17 placement matchups; pair them by hand if Sleeper does not
- [ ] Confirm payout eligibility ([§8.8](08-trades.md#88-the-annual-trade-requirement)) before distributing
- [ ] Distribute payouts within 14 days of the championship
